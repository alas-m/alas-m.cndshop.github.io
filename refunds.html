Refund & Withdrawal Policy

Last updated: 2026 · Two regimes: ready-made products vs custom projects & services

Shrnutí (čeština): Hotové produkty z regálu (digitální obsah, zdrojový kód) jsou po doručení nevratné — soubory nelze smazat z druhé strany. Zakázkové projekty a služby vratné jsou: pokud ještě nezačaly, vracíme plnou cenu; pokud začaly, vracíme poměrnou část za neprovedenou práci. Spotřebitelské právo odstoupit do 14 dnů u digitálního obsahu dodaného s vaším souhlasem zaniká (§ 1837 písm. l) občanského zákoníku).

1. Two regimes, clearly separated

  • Ready-made products (“shelf” items on the shop page — Noctis, Blackwell, Mimesis, Kataxis and similar, already built and sold as-is): delivered as files; see section 2.
  • Custom projects & services (development, research, analysis done to order): see section 3.

2. Ready-made products: non-refundable after delivery

Ready-made digital products are not refundable after delivery. The reason is simple: once the files are sent, they cannot be recalled or reliably deleted from the buyer’s side — a “change of mind” refund would leave us with no way to take the product back. Accordingly:

  • Before sending files we ask for your express consent that immediate delivery starts and that the EU 14-day withdrawal right ends once delivery begins (§ 1837 písm. l) of the Czech Civil Code).
  • After files have been delivered following that consent, no refunds are granted for change of mind.
  • If you change your mind before the files are sent, we simply do not send them and refund the full amount.
  • The only post-delivery refund for a ready-made product is when it is defective or does not match its product-page description (section 4).

3. Custom projects & services: refundable

Custom work is a service contract, and it remains refundable under these rules:

  • Work not started: full refund — just cancel in the chat.
  • Work in progress: you may stop at any time. We refund the proportionate part for the work not yet performed (you pay only for the work actually done up to that point).
  • Delivered custom project: you may still request a refund within the 14-day window — unlike shelf products, custom work is refundable. We process it case-by-case and may deduct only the proportionate value of the work performed (files already delivered do not block a refund by themselves).
  • Scope, price and delivery terms are agreed in the chat before work starts; that agreement defines what “per specification” means.

4. Defective or not-as-described deliverables (both regimes)

If what you receive does not match what was promised (missing core files, unusable as described, custom build not matching the agreed specification), we will first fix or re-deliver it; if that is not possible within a reasonable time, you receive a full refund. This is in addition to, not instead of, your statutory rights.

5. How to request a refund or withdrawal

Send an unambiguous statement by any means — a Telegram message or an e-mail is enough. You may use this template:

EN: “To CND Shop — I request a refund / withdraw from my contract for: [product or project, order date]. Reason (optional): [reason]. Name: [your name]. Contact: [your Telegram/e-mail]. Date: [date].”

CZ: „Oznamuji, že odstupuji od smlouvy / žádám o vrácení platby za: [produkt/zakázka, datum]. Jméno: [jméno]. Kontakt: [Telegram/e-mail]. Datum: [datum].“

Send it to or . We confirm receipt immediately. For valid refunds we reimburse within 14 days using the same payment method, with no fees.

6. What is not refundable

  • Ready-made digital products after delivery (files cannot be recalled) — except when defective or not as described (section 4).
  • Work already performed on a custom project (only the unperformed portion is refunded; the performed portion is not).

7. Disputes

Contact us first — we answer fast. EU consumers may use the EU ODR platform (ec.europa.eu/consumers/odr); Czech consumers may contact the Czech Trade Inspection Authority (coi.cz) for out-of-court resolution.
