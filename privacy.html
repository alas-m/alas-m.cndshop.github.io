2. What this website does not do

  • No forms. There is no contact/order form on this website, so no personal data is submitted through it. If a form is ever added, it will only be processed after you tick an unticked consent checkbox (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 7) stating exactly what is collected and why — no pre-ticked boxes, no bundled consent.
  • No cookies, no analytics, no tracking. This is a static site; it sets no cookies and loads no analytics or advertising scripts. (Icon fonts are loaded from a CDN; see section 6.)
  • No account system. We never create profiles of visitors.

4. Retention

  • Correspondence and order records: kept as long as needed for tax, warranty/refund and limitation periods (typically up to the applicable statutory period, e.g. Czech tax retention rules for business records), then deleted.

5. Hosting (GitHub Pages)

This website is hosted by GitHub, Inc. on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes standard technical data (such as IP addresses and request logs) in accordance with its own privacy statement, which applies to that processing: GitHub General Privacy Statement. We do not receive or control GitHub’s server logs. Public repositories and Pages content are public by nature — do not put personal data of others into any file you publish here.

6. Third-party links and fonts

Buttons on this site open Telegram; Telegram’s own privacy policy applies once you are there. Icon fonts are served from a CDN (cdnjs); a request to that CDN may reveal your IP address to it, governed by the CDN provider’s policy. We do not read that data.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to: access, rectification, erasure (“right to be forgotten”), restriction of processing, data portability, and to object to processing based on legitimate interest. You may withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent. To exercise a right, write to or — we respond within one month.

You may lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority of your country of residence. For customers in Czechia this is the Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů (Czech DPA): uoou.gov.cz.

8. Children

The shop is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly process data of minors. If you believe a minor’s data reached us, contact us and we will delete it.
